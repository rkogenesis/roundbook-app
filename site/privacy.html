Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

RoundBook is an offline notebook app for doctors to keep track of their own patients. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What we collect

Nothing. RoundBook has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere by itself. The developer never receives, sees or stores any information you enter.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter (patient details, clinical notes, lab results, photos and any other records) is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database (AES-256). The encryption key is protected by your device's Android Keystore. Photos are stored encrypted too. Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for RoundBook.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to:

  • Exports (CSV/ZIP), PDFs, handover sheets and shared text: you pick the app or place they go to. Exports can be masked or de-identified to remove patient identifiers.
  • Encrypted backups (.rbk): saved to a folder you choose (for example one synced to your own cloud storage). They can only be opened with the password you set.
  • "Read with AI app" (off by default): sends a photo of a lab report, OPD card or round note to an app you pick, together with fixed reading instructions (which contain no patient details and are also copied to your clipboard so you can paste them). RoundBook asks for confirmation every time. What that app does with the photo is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Document scanner: the camera scanning screen is provided by Google Play services on your device. RoundBook receives only the scanned image.

Your responsibilities

You are the person collecting and handling patient information. You are responsible for following your institution's policies, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and any other law that applies to you, including consent where it is required, and for anything you export or share.

Deleting data

You can delete any record in the app (deleted items stay in Trash for 30 days). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes everything on the device; copies you exported or backed up are not affected.

This website

This page is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.

Children

RoundBook is meant for medical professionals and is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and shown in the app.
